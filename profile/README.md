## Engineering Principles

**Evidence before conclusions.**

Document assumptions and validate claims against observable results.

**Reproducibility by design.**

Provide the methods and materials necessary to repeat experiments.

**Production as the standard.**

Consider reliability, security, integration, and operational constraints.

**Open technical discourse.**

Invite scrutiny, discussion, and contributions from the engineering community.
